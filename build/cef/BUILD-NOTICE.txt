Playport's CEF 143.0.14 low-address-space build (decision 0110)

This payload is for local use on the owner's phone only. It is not yet
approved for distribution. CEF and Chromium's notices accompany it.
FFmpeg is built with Chromium branding and proprietary codecs disabled;
its licence summary and LGPL-2.1 text accompany it. Corresponding Source
packaging and the rebuild/relink route are tracked in PLA-372 and must be
completed before any release or tester copy.

libcef.dll and chrome_elf.dll statically link the MSVC CRT under the Visual
Studio licence accepted by the owner. The absence of separate Microsoft
redistributable DLLs does not mean these binaries contain no Microsoft code.
No Microsoft SDK, toolchain or separate redistributable DLL is supplied here.
