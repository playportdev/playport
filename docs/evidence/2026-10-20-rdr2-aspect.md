# RDR2's stretched 3D: where the camera's aspect comes from (2026-10-20)

PLA-364, PLA-413. On the phone Red Dead Redemption 2 (Steam 1174180) draws its 3D world stretched about 3.5 times
horizontally about the centre, with the vertical field of view and the HUD right
([desktop reference](2026-10-20-rdr2-desktop-reference.md)): its camera projects with an aspect of about 0.507
where the desktop's is 1.778. The upscale and the presentation are ruled out
([proc-socket-table](2026-10-20-rdr2-proc-socket-table.md), run s5).

## The instruments (dev IPA `a6ca0948`)

- **`patches/madeira-unix` 0178**: runtime key `env.MADEIRA_DISPLAY_QUERY_LOG=1` logs, per process, each distinct
  answer of GetSystemMetrics, GetMonitorInfo, the primary-monitor and virtual-screen rectangles,
  EnumDisplaySettings, EnumDisplayDevices, GetDisplayConfigBufferSizes, QueryDisplayConfig,
  DisplayConfigGetDeviceInfo and the display DC's GetDeviceCaps (`[disp-q]` lines).
- **`patches/wine-unix` 0044**: with the same key, the Vulkan surface's driver extents and the extents the game is
  given (`[vk-surf]`).
- **`patches/mesa` 0063**: Graphics option `MESA_KK_FRAME_PROBE_S=N`: every N s after the process's first draw, ten
  times at most, the next 2500 draws' `[kk-draw]` lines (render area, attachment, viewport, scissor), and a scan of
  every host-visible allocation for perspective view-projection matrices, the desktop reference's method
  (`[kk-proj] P00 P11 aspect hits`).
- Hollow Knight first frame+10 passed on `a6ca0948` (`ui-runs/20261020T142119`).

## Run f1 (`ui-runs/20261020T142311-rdr2-f1`, IPA `a6ca0948`): not a result

- The footprint reached 7,020 MB at +125.1 s, before the game's sign-in (+141.4 s), and 0130's memory end ended
  the launcher and its Social Club helpers; the game then showed "Not signed in". Nothing was pressed there, and the
  run was ended. Run s4 (the "Buy" screen) had the same order: the memory end at 7,027 MB, +133.8 s, the sign-in at
  +152.5 s. In s5 and s6 the footprint at the sign-in was 5.5–5.6 GB. Decision 0140 moves the end before the
  sign-in to the limit less 400 MB.
- f1's footprint before the sign-in ran about 1.3 GB above s6's at the same stage, with the game's own
  pseudo-process at an earlier point of its load (private 854 MB against 2,243 MB at s6's sign-in): the difference is
  outside the per-process accounting. Open; not chased.
- **What RDR2.exe asked of the display**, once each (`[disp-q]`): EnumDisplayDevices (`\\.\DISPLAY1`), GetMonitorInfo
  (0,0–1280,720, primary), EnumDisplaySettings current 1280×720 at 120 Hz and the mode list 640×480, 800×600,
  960×540, 1280×720 at 32/16/8 bpp, **GetDisplayConfigBufferSizes: 1 path, 2 modes; QueryDisplayConfig: 0 paths,
  0 modes** (asked twice, the second time with room for 256); **DisplayConfigGetDeviceInfo GET_SOURCE_NAME:
  failed**; GetDeviceCaps HORZRES/VERTRES-class 1280/720, LOGPIXELS 96; no SM_CXSCREEN or SM_CXMAXTRACK.
  Desktop Wine and Windows answer QueryDisplayConfig with the monitor's path. In the virtual-monitor regime the
  path loop skips the virtual monitor (it has no source), so the game got a count of one and then nothing.
- `[vk-surf]`: the driver's surface is 2736×1260 (transform identity), the game is given 1280×720.

## Run f2 (`ui-runs/20261020T143924-rdr2-f2`, IPA `7cd89034`, decision 0140): signed in, then stuck

- The game signed in at +113.5 s with the footprint at 7,018 MB, so decision 0133's dev end of the game's own
  Social Club came in the same one-second slice, 1.2 s after its Social Club log said it had signed in online. The
  helper's channels broke, its log went to `FAILED` and `Signed out.`, 0138 refused two later helper starts, and
  the game sat on a black loading screen for 8 minutes; the run was ended. Decision 0141: that end now waits at
  least 120 s after the sign-in (144–155 s in s5, s6 and e3b, which played).
- **The projection scan** (`[kk-proj]`, Resolution Scale Off, landing page and loading screens, five probes): the
  most frequent perspective matrix has **P11 2.0278 and P00 12.1668, aspect 0.1667** (12,819 hits), next to
  2D/UI transforms (P00/P11 2/1, 2/2). P11 is within 1 % of the desktop's 2.042, so this is a camera with the
  desktop's vertical field of view, projected 6.0 times too wide. The desktop reference measured ×3.50 at
  Resolution Scale 1/2 from the cabin; s6's cabin shots at Off look far wider than s2's at 1/2.

## Run f3 (`ui-runs/20261020T145826-rdr2-f3`, IPA `0fec3324…87ce`, madeira-unix 0179, decisions 0140, 0141): fixed

- **What RDR2.exe was given now** (`[disp-q]`): QueryDisplayConfig 1 path, source 0 at 1280×720, target 0
  active 1280×720, rotation identity; DisplayConfigGetDeviceInfo GET_SOURCE_NAME and GET_TARGET_NAME succeed.
- **The window is no longer centred on a 1920×1080 desktop:** the guest window shows the desktop at (0,0) 1280×720
  (`[guest-window]`), where s1, s2, r3 and f1 had it at (317,167).
- **The projection** (`[kk-proj]`, two probes, Resolution Scale Off, Story gameplay on the porch): **P00 1.1406,
  P11 2.0278, aspect 1.7778** (5,356 and 12,798 hits). Against the desktop's P00 1.1486 / P11 2.0420 that is a
  horizontal scale of 1.1406/1.1486 = **×0.99** and the same aspect (1.7778), where f2 had P00 12.1668 (×10.6
  at Off) and the desktop fit of s1/s2 gave ×3.50 at 1/2. The P11 of both runs is 0.7 % under the desktop's.
- **The owner, watching the phone during f3: "The rendering I see on the phone looks fixed."** The f3 screenshots
  (in the run directory) show Arthur on the cabin's porch in normal proportions.
- The save loaded on the porch, not in the cabin of the desktop reference, so no screenshot fit was made; the
  projection is the measure.
- **So the camera's aspect came from the display configuration.** In the virtual-monitor regime
  GetDisplayConfigBufferSizes counted one path and QueryDisplayConfig returned none; RDR2 then sized its window as for
  a 1920×1080 desktop and projected with a wrong aspect (0.507 at 1/2, 1/6 at Off).
- f3 itself ended in a memory kill: the game signed in at +106 s (5,919 MB, the launcher ended), 0141 held
  0133's end until +255 s, when a burst had taken the footprint from 6,865 to 7,958 MB; it peaked at 8,118 MB
  and iOS ended the app (`per-process-limit`) at about +362 s, after 2–3 minutes of Story. The run also had the
  projection scans (MESA_KK_FRAME_PROBE_S=120), which read every host-visible allocation; later runs leave them off.
- Hollow Knight first frame+10 passed on `0fec3324` (`ui-runs/20261020T145656`).
