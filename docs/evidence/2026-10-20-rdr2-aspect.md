# RDR2's stretched 3D: where the camera's aspect comes from (2026-10-20)

PLA-364, PLA-413. On the phone Red Dead Redemption 2 (Steam 1174180) draws its 3D world stretched about 3.5 times
horizontally about the centre, with the vertical field of view and the HUD right
([desktop reference](2026-10-20-rdr2-desktop-reference.md)): its camera projects with an aspect of about 0.507
where the desktop's is 1.778. The upscale and the presentation are ruled out
([proc-socket-table](2026-10-20-rdr2-proc-socket-table.md), run s5).

## The instruments (dev IPA `a6ca0948`)

- **`patches/madeira-unix` 0178**: runtime key `env.MADEIRA_DISPLAY_QUERY_LOG=1` logs, per process, each distinct
  answer of GetSystemMetrics, GetMonitorInfo, the primary-monitor and virtual-screen rectangles,
  EnumDisplaySettings, EnumDisplayDevices, GetDisplayConfigBufferSizes, QueryDisplayConfig,
  DisplayConfigGetDeviceInfo and the display DC's GetDeviceCaps (`[disp-q]` lines).
- **`patches/wine-unix` 0044**: with the same key, the Vulkan surface's driver extents and the extents the game is
  given (`[vk-surf]`).
- **`patches/mesa` 0063**: Graphics option `MESA_KK_FRAME_PROBE_S=N`: every N s after the process's first draw, ten
  times at most, the next 2500 draws' `[kk-draw]` lines (render area, attachment, viewport, scissor), and a scan of
  every host-visible allocation for perspective view-projection matrices, the desktop reference's method
  (`[kk-proj] P00 P11 aspect hits`).
- Hollow Knight first frame+10 passed on `a6ca0948` (`ui-runs/20261020T142119`).
