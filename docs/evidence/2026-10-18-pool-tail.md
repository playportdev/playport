# A less greedy JIT pool tail for the Rockstar launcher's Open (PLA-394)

**Linear:** [PLA-394](https://linear.app/playportdev/issue/PLA-394),
[PLA-370](https://linear.app/playportdev/issue/PLA-370).
**Plan:** [low-VA CEF](../plans/2026-10-16-cef-low-va-build.md).
**Change:** `patches/madeira-unix` 0157, PlayportKit `PublisherLauncher.runtime`, decision
[0116](../decisions/0116-tail-room-for-every-process-and-the-launchers-smc-checks.md).
Branch `dev/pla-291-rockstar-launcher`.

**Question:** The launcher's Opens with fex 0047 ([evidence](2026-10-18-fex-exit-fault.md)) ran
the 896 MiB pool to its end in every run: tail 416–433 MiB, head 460–464 MiB. Where does the tail
go, and what makes it smaller without taking a single game's code buffer?

## Where the tail went

From the host logs (`$PLAYPORT_BUILD/ui-runs/<run>/pull/s1-host.log`): `tail EC_CODE`, `tail
REUSE`, `tail BIG`, `tail CAP`, `[jit-pool] tail of pid` and FEX's `[gen] alloc#` lines, process
names from `NtCreateUserProcess` lines. Run `20261018T095950` (SMC checks off, cache default,
black window, `tail_fatal`):

| Process | Tail it held at the end | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seven early processes (services, Launcher.exe, Rockstar Service and the others alive) | 7 × (16 KiB + 16 MiB) = 112 MiB | each process's dispatcher carve and first code buffer; none filled it |
| Social Club browser (pid 01e0) | 16 + 128 MiB | filled its 16 MiB, then `tail BIG: granting 0x8000000` with head 335 MiB, tail 112 MiB |
| first renderer (pid 02b4) | 16 + 16 + 128 MiB | its 16 KiB ask was served a free 16 MiB carve (`tail REUSE … size=0x1000000 (asked 0x4000)`), its first buffer was a fresh 16 MiB, and the second 128 MiB was granted with head 403 MiB, tail 256 MiB: 189 MiB of room, 61 MiB left after it |
| second renderer (pid 0308) | 16 (served for a 16 KiB ask) + 4 × 16 MiB | every 128 MiB ask was refused to 16 MiB (`tail CAP`); 24 generations in a few seconds, 3–4 live at once while threads still held the old one (`prev_use_count` ≈ 20) |

Total 432 MiB (`[pool-tail] TOTALS carves=20 live=0x1b01c000`), every carve live. The head then
reached 463 MiB, head and tail met, and the next 16 MiB to 1 MiB asks were refused (`TAIL REFUSED`,
one fatal). `093540` and `094035` show the same grants (two `tail BIG` 128 MiB at tail 112 and
256 MiB) and the same refusals of the second renderer; they reached the sign-in page with a few
MiB to spare.

Dead processes' tail is reclaimed: their carves come back through `tail REUSE` (the first Launcher.exe's
carves served LauncherPatcher, the first RockstarErrorHandler's the second Launcher.exe). The tail cursor itself never moves down, so its peak is what processes alive at once
held. Neither 128 MiB buffer was filled during the run (no further `[gen] alloc` from either
process).

The lever is therefore the cap's room in a session of many processes: the second 128 MiB grant
took the room the head's late images and the second renderer needed. A smaller first buffer for
every process would save more but changes FEX for every title; the cap rule alone keeps a lone
game's buffer.

## Change

- `patches/madeira-unix` 0157: the cap's room also keeps 16 MiB for each other process holding a
  live tail carve; replayed on `095950`'s numbers, the browser still gets 128 MiB (7 others: room
  289 MiB) and the first renderer 32 MiB (8 others: room 61 MiB), 96 MiB less. An ask below
  16 MiB no longer takes a free 16 MiB carve while a fresh carve fits: 32 MiB less in `095950`.
  Hollow Knight (two processes, head 81 MiB, tail 32 MiB of 512) keeps its 128 MiB buffer.
- `PublisherLauncher.rockstar.runtime = ["env.FEX_SMCCHECKS": "none"]`, under saved keys of the
  same name: clearing the launcher's settings no longer turns the checks back on (`095302`
  without the key: tail 561 MiB, CEF crash dialog).

head is the next lever, not the tail.
