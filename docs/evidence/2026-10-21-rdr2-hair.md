# RDR2 hair, beard, brim and ear: a Metal capture of the cabin frame (PLA-364)

**Linear:** PLA-364. **IPA:** dev `Playport-26.5-41431327.ipa`, sha256
`414313273a71b9841c0d70a93f9fe7e68d1e023a19c80e94453accf46eed5957` (built from `5257e75`, installed;
the checkout was at `96660ea`). **Run:** `$PLAYPORT_BUILD/gpu-runs/20261021T225337-rdr2-hair`, one
`pp phone lock` session: `pp gpu capture --title app-1174180 --settings
'{"graphics":"vulkan","aspect":"16:9","runtime":"","graphicsOptions":""}' --frame 10800 --secs 540
--expect-gb 1.5`, with Story pressed once on the landing (decision 0156: no box came).

**Result: the capture did not finish, so it gives no frame to compare with the desktop reference.**
The scene and the defect were captured, but none of the bundle's closing files were written.

## 1. The run

- **Phone at the start:** battery 80 %, on power, 5.2 GB free. Before the run, Playport's own MSL dump
  cache `Library/Caches/kk-dump` (3,567 files, 209,715,441 bytes, already pulled to the workstation)
  was deleted to make room.
- **Timeline:**
  - first frame at +85.6 s;
  - Story pressed at about first frame +49 s;
  - `[frames] n=10800` and `[kk] frame 10800: A new capture will be saved to …` at first frame +311 s,
    about 260 s after Story, in the cabin.
- **After the capture began,** one `[kk-gpu]` line followed: 12 frames in 18.3 s with
  `submit=17877.7 ms`, so the submits were blocked in Metal's capture. No further `[kk-gpu]` line came,
  and no `[kk] frame 10800: capture ended`.
- **The end:** the play stopped at first frame +540 s (`after_s=524`) while the capture was still open.
  Free space was then 2.5 GB.
- **The bundle** was pulled in full: 1,653 files, 5,239,259,136 bytes, 213,457 links.
  - The first, serial pull was stopped after 20 minutes at about 1 small file or link per second over
    Wi-Fi. The supervisor's parallel `afc.py pull-bundle` (`62bd556`) then resumed it in 233 s.
  - The bundle on the phone is deleted by the supervisor with the checked counts. That deletion is not
    part of this note.

## 2. What the bundle holds

| Present | Missing |
| --- | --- |
| 1,649 `MTLHeap-*` files (81 of 32 MiB, 1,094 of 128 KiB, 474 smaller); 3 drawable textures (BGRA8 1280×720, IOSurface); a 4 MB `store0` | `capture`, `index`, `metadata`, `device-resources-*`, `unused-device-resources`, the shader binaries |

- **`pp gpu read`** stops at the missing `index`. No pass, draw, pipeline, depth/stencil state or
  target format of the phone's frame can be read.
- **Why the closing files are missing:** `mtl_stop_gpu_capture` (`patches/mesa` 0017) writes them
  synchronously at the next present. Either it had not returned when the play ended about 245 s later,
  or it ran out of space: the files hold 5.24 GB against 5.2 GB free. The phone lost about 2.7 GB, so
  the heap files are likely sparse there. Which of the two happened is not known.
- **The size of a capture:** a gameplay capture of RDR2 is about 4 times the landing's 1.33 GB. KosmicKrisp's
  `[kk-mem] bos=` figure (about 2.5 GB in the cabin) underestimates it. A complete capture needs about
  8.5–9 GB free on the phone, plus a `--secs` that outlasts the write.
- **The drawables** hold recently presented frames. Decoded as linear BGRA, they show the cabin with
  Arthur, a saturated white patch on the hat brim and a bright red patch at the ear: the defect, in
  the scene that was meant to be captured. The images stay under `.work` because they show the game's
  artwork.
- **The heaps** hold the previous frame's targets. They are in Metal's GPU-optimized layout
  (KosmicKrisp sets `allowGPUOptimizedContents` for every optimal-tiling image), and their offsets
  are unknown without `device-resources`, so they were not decoded.

## 3. The phone's shaders compared with the desktop's (host only)

The SPIR-V of the phone's earlier KosmicKrisp MSL dumps (same game build) matches the desktop
RenderDoc capture's SPIR-V byte for byte for these shaders:
- the head, hat, body and hair G-buffer shaders;
- the lighting passes 15948, 15952 and 16038;
- the head forward redraw 16126;
- TAA + tonemap 17063.

The fog, hair-resolve and mask shaders were below the dump's size threshold and were not dumped.
So the phone runs the desktop's own shaders, and any difference comes from translation or Metal state.

What the MSL of the lighting passes, the redraw and TAA shows:
- **Precision:** colour maths is `float` throughout. The only `half` values are KosmicKrisp's
  sampler LOD fields.
- **Blending:** 16038 blends in the shader as `light + dst.rgb` and keeps `dst.a`.
- **Stencil:** the class is read from component `.x` of the stencil view (`& 8` kill, `& 7`), as on
  the desktop.
- **Math mode:** fast math, with per-instruction `safe`/`relaxed` pragmas. `MESA_KK_SAFE_MATH` was
  already ruled out on the phone.

Nothing there explains an R-only overflow or a white brim.

## 4. Leads after this run

1. **Untested:** one colour channel overflowing or non-finite in the HDR target or the TAA history.
   It needs the targets' values at the ear and brim after each lighting pass, which this capture could
   not give.
2. **Untested:** the GPU-optimized (losslessly compressed) layout of render targets that the shaders
   read back as `[[color(0)]]`. KosmicKrisp already turns that layout off for feedback-loop images and
   for E5B9G9R9 on Apple family 10, which includes this A19 phone.
3. `LEADS.md` (a)–(d): unchanged.

**No fix is proposed.** The next step is to dump the frame's targets rather than capture it:
- a dev-only mesa switch that, at present N, copies each 640×360 RGBA16Float/R11G11B10Float target
  and the stencil after every render pass to files (tens of MB);
- one A/B play with optimized layouts off.
