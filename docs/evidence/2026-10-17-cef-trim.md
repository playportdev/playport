# Fewer Chromium threads and processes for the Rockstar launcher's Open (PLA-384)

**Linear:** [PLA-384](https://linear.app/playportdev/issue/PLA-384),
[PLA-370](https://linear.app/playportdev/issue/PLA-370).
**Plan:** [low-VA CEF](../plans/2026-10-16-cef-low-va-build.md).
**Decision:** [0114](../decisions/0114-trim-social-clubs-chromium-and-the-launchers-threads.md).
Branch `dev/pla-291-rockstar-launcher`.

**Question:** Can a smaller Chromium in our CEF, with a quieter launcher, fit the Open in FEX's
16 GiB host band and the 896 MiB JIT pool? The CEF changes are `patches/chromium` 0008–0013;
the launcher's is `PublisherLauncher.rockstar.graphicsEnvironment`. FEX is unchanged, with
16 MiB call-return stacks.

## Desktop Proton

**Setup.** One run, `trim384-sway`, in the scratch prefix:

- under sway's Xwayland (`DISPLAY=:0`, `WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-1`);
- with the steamclient overrides off and an empty fake Steam directory;
- with 0109 and 0111's switches, for 150 s.

No prefix process was running before or after the run. Social Club's 81 original files were
restored and verified afterwards. The comparison is PLA-381's run, `audio381-sway`.

| | PLA-381 (`audio381-sway`) | PLA-384 (`trim384-sway`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chromium processes | 5: browser, storage, 3 renderers | 4: browser, 3 renderers |
| utility processes started | storage, ProcessorMetrics, FilePatcher, Unzipper ×2 | none |
| browser threads (peak) | 68 | 57 |
| renderer threads (peak) | 33 / 31 / 30 | 21 / 18 / 17 |
| storage utility threads | 9 | — |
| CEF threads (incl. the 1-thread wrapper) | 171 | 112 |
| CEF PSS | 2,028 MiB | 1,729 MiB |
| Social Club `FINISHED` | 2.2 s | 2.5 s |
| launcher `WaitForWindow → Active` | yes | yes, 0.7 s after `WaitForWindow` |
| sign-in form at 30–150 s | visible | visible |

- The screenshots at 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 s show the Sign In form (Email, Password) with the
  cookie notice over its lower part, as before.
- The utilities came from the browser's command lines in `wine.log`. FilePatcher and Unzipper are
  Rockstar's or Social Club's update work. This run started neither; this record does not claim
  why.
- Desktop Proton runs its own DXVK, so the launcher's DXVK and vkd3d-proton settings could not be
  checked here.
- Rockstar's logs and the screenshots stay in the build area
  (`agent-notes/pla254-rockstar/runs/trim384-sway/`).
