# Fewer Chromium threads and processes for the Rockstar launcher's Open (PLA-384)

**Linear:** [PLA-384](https://linear.app/playportdev/issue/PLA-384),
[PLA-370](https://linear.app/playportdev/issue/PLA-370).
**Plan:** [low-VA CEF](../plans/finished.md#own-low-address-space-cef).
**Decision:** [0114](../decisions/0114-trim-social-clubs-chromium-and-the-launchers-threads.md).
Branch `dev/pla-291-rockstar-launcher`.

**Question:** Can a smaller Chromium in our CEF, with a quieter launcher, fit the Open in FEX's
16 GiB host band and the 896 MiB JIT pool? The CEF changes are `patches/chromium` 0008–0013;
the launcher's is `PublisherLauncher.rockstar.graphicsEnvironment`. FEX is unchanged, with
16 MiB call-return stacks.

**The pool's head still runs out [M]:**

- The first exhaustion came 52 s after the game started. The band peaked at 70 s.
- The first refusal was a third renderer's `xtajit64.dll` copy (`want=0x498000`).
- After the main tree was up (browser, 2 renderers, the launchers), these started and died with a
  single thread:
  - 8 more renderers;
  - 10 `unzip.mojom.Unzipper` utilities (Rockstar's or Social Club's update work);
  - one `on_device_model` utility;
  - 19 `rpcss.exe` / 18 `dllhost.exe` pairs (`194000` had 24 / 24).
- The browser, its 2 renderers and both Launcher.exe processes kept running to the end.

**Screenshots [M]:**

- 30 s: Playport's launch screen.
- 45 s: the launcher's "Connecting to Rockstar Games Services" window, with its text drawn.
- 60, 75, 90, 120, 150 and 240 s and at stop, all identical: Social Club's sign-in form on its
  Rockstar background. Shown are the logo, the white form with two input fields (the password
  field's eye icon), a checkbox, a settings button, and the cookie panel with its two buttons and
  close mark.
- **No text is drawn in the page.** The "Sign In" heading, the field labels, the cookie notice
  and the button labels are missing. On desktop Proton the same payload draws them.
- So the sign-in form is on the phone's screen for the first time, but it cannot be used.
- The renderers logged no DirectWrite or font error. The text is a separate problem, not studied
  here.

## Reading

- **The trim worked.**
  - The Open no longer fills the band: 168 threads at most, against 202, and no refusal.
  - Two Chromium processes are gone.
  - The launcher's window controller reaches `Active`.
  - The form stays on screen through 240 s.
- **The JIT pool's head is still the limit** for whatever starts after the main tree. Each later
  child failed on its own builtin copies.
- **Next:**
  - why the page draws no text on the phone;
  - whether the late renderers, the Unzipper utilities and the rpcss/dllhost pairs matter. They
    started after the form, and the form does not need them.
- **Where the logs are.** Rockstar's text logs are in the build area
  (`agent-notes/pla370-cef/inputs/pla384-rockstar-logs/`; `launcher.log` holds a public IP). The
  run directories hold the screenshots and the host log.
