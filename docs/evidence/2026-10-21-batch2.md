## 2. Not done, and why

- **FEX #4, inline icache flush.** The review requires understanding the old ILL-at-64-byte-boundary
  crash that fex-port 0028's syscall flush fixed; neither the history read here nor the runs explain it,
  so the syscall stays.
- **FEX #3b, remembering backpatched sites.** The 39 k `UNALIGNED-BACKPATCH` events per run are Wine's
  Mach handler's (signal_arm64_ios.c), not FEX's. Teaching the JIT to emit the half-barrier form for those
  guest RIPs needs Wine to pass them to each pseudo-process's FEX, and ml613/ml614 record that native code
  cannot call FEX's EC exports: a new channel, not a small patch. And the gain is unknown: a site faults
  once and is patched in place, so only recompiles of it pay again, and no run counts them.
- **KK #1 (mesa 0092) is the shared-allocator design, not the per-command-buffer one** that failed in
  ded9a76a; it relies on Vulkan's rule that a command buffer is not pending when reset, freed or begun
  again, and on the queue's re-record buffers being reset after completion under its mutex.
- **KK #5 pass descriptor reuse and the rest of the default-state skip, #7 autorelease pools, #9 bigger
  upload buffers with a device free list.** The pass descriptor and state shadow touch most of
  BeginRendering and the dynamic-state flush; dropping `@autoreleasepool` from setters leaks whatever
  Metal autoreleases on Wine's pool-less threads until they exit, which the host mock cannot show; 1 MiB
  upload buffers with today's 32 kept per pool would hold up to 32 MiB idle per pool, against the jetsam
  margin, and a device-wide free list is a new locking design. Left for a later batch.

## 4. The combined phone test

One RDR2 run with all three keys, after the Hollow Knight gate:

    env.MADEIRA_FEX_BATCH2=1 env.MESA_KK_BATCH2=1 env.PLAYPORT_SC_BLOCK_CRASHPAD=1

Check, besides frame rate against the last defaults run: the three startup lines above in every
process; no `thread_get_register_pointer_values not supported` FIXME (it would mean `dmb` has no barrier:
skip `dmb`); `[sc-crashpad]` refusals and the launcher and game going on without their reporter; the
fault paths' `[callret]` line, now in the 1 MiB window's terms. On a failure, bisect with
`MADEIRA_FEX_BATCH2_SKIP=` (alias, ffs, dmb, uabp) and `MESA_KK_BATCH2_SKIP=` (root, map, residency, alloc, ampl, oq),
or drop `PLAYPORT_SC_BLOCK_CRASHPAD`. The fixes have no
switch (fex 0070, mesa 0090/0093, madeira-unix 0210/0211) and need the Hollow Knight gate anyway; mesa
0090's occlusion Accumulate and 0093's fence are the ones a rendering difference would point to.
