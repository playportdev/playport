# Playport 0.3.3: JIT from StikDebug or LiveContainer

Playport can now get JIT the old way too, from StikDebug or from LiveContainer,
as well as from its built-in helper.

## Changes
- **JIT method.** Settings › Setup check › JIT method (or X on the setup
  checklist's first step) picks where a game's JIT comes from:
  - **Built-in** (the default): Playport's own helper, as before.
  - **StikDebug**: Play opens StikDebug, which enables JIT with its
    `universal.js` script and comes back to Playport.
  - **Another app**: Play waits for JIT from another app, such as
    LiveContainer's *Launch with JIT*, with the `universal.js` script.
- **LiveContainer.** Inside LiveContainer, Built-in is not offered: LiveContainer
  cannot start Playport's JIT helper. Set LiveContainer's JIT enabler to StikDebug
  with `universal.js`, launch Playport with JIT, and Play uses it at once. Or choose
  StikDebug in Playport and turn on LiveContainer's *Use LiveContainer's Bundle ID*.
- **After a game.** With JIT from another app, Playport's pairing and LocalDevVPN
  are no longer setup steps. Without a pairing, or inside LiveContainer, Playport
  cannot restart itself after a game: it asks you to close it and launch it again.
- **Not tested on the phone yet** with StikDebug or LiveContainer: Playport is
  tested on one phone, which has neither. Built-in was checked. If you play with
  StikDebug or LiveContainer, please tell us how it goes.

Everything else is as in 0.3.2.

## Install

Download `Playport-0.3.3.ipa` and sideload it with your own Apple ID, keeping the
increased memory limit and the JIT helper extension. If you have an earlier version,
install over it: your games, saves and Steam login stay. Bring your own games from
Steam.

The complete Corresponding Source of this exact build, the notices and the
rebuild instructions are attached as `Playport-0.3.3-source.tar`, `NOTICES.tar`
and `INSTALL-REBUILD.tar`. `SHA256SUMS` covers every file.
